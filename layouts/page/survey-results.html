What this does not tell you

  • These are devices, not people. A value's number is how many distinct browsers chose it. Answering again from the same browser changes nothing, which stops anyone stuffing the numbers — but it also means two people sharing one computer count as one, and one person on a phone and a laptop counts as two. It under-counts as readily as it over-counts.
  • No combinations. The counts are kept one value at a time, so the most interesting question — which values people hold together — cannot be answered from this data at all. That is the price of storing nothing about anybody.
  • No map yet. The original idea was geographic. There is not enough data for that to be anything but a picture of noise.
  • Not a sample of the world. It is a sample of people who found this page, which is a much narrower and stranger group.
